Office of the Controller of Examinations

The Office of the Controller of Examinations is responsible for planning, conducting and managing all university examinations with fairness, transparency and efficiency. It ensures timely declaration of results, maintenance of academic records, issue of marksheets, and implementation of examination policies.

This portal serves as the official platform for examination-related announcements, notices and academic updates for students and faculty.

About Imperial University

Imperial University of Delhi is committed to providing quality education, academic excellence, and a student-centric learning environment. The university offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma programmes across various disciplines while maintaining high standards in teaching, research, and innovation.

The Imperial University Result Portal has been developed by the Office of the Controller of Examinations to provide students with secure and convenient access to their semester results, provisional marksheets, examination notifications, and other academic information. Faculty members and administrators can efficiently manage examination records, ensuring a transparent, accurate, and timely result publication process.
